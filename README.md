# g290-best-meal

Entry for make.fun **The Best Meal You Ever Had**
(`u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85`).

`main.ts` writes one sentence about the best meal ever had to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My grandmother ladled soto ayam over rice at her chipped Formica table, steam fogging her glasses, and it is still the warmest thing anyone has ever handed me.

159 characters, 1 sentence, 1 line. It names a real dish (soto ayam) and a real
place (her chipped Formica table), carries two concrete sensory details (steam
fogging her glasses, the ladle over rice), belongs to exactly one person's
memory, and lands on an image — the warmest thing anyone has ever handed me —
that stays after a single reading.

Run: `node main.ts` (Node 22+, no dependencies).
